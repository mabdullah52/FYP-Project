title: Breathing exercises and staying active
source: Summary of general pulmonary rehabilitation patient education (American Lung Association and British Lung Foundation style guidance). Check with your doctor before starting new exercise.

## Pursed-lip breathing
Breathe in slowly through your nose for about 2 seconds. Purse your lips as if you were going to whistle and breathe out gently through them for about 4 seconds. Repeat for a few minutes. This slows your breathing and helps when you feel short of breath.

## Belly (diaphragmatic) breathing
Sit or lie comfortably with one hand on your chest and one on your belly. Breathe in through your nose so that your belly rises while your chest stays still. Breathe out slowly through pursed lips. Practise 5 to 10 minutes, two or three times a day.

## Staying active
Regular walking helps your lungs and heart. Start with a short walk of 5 to 10 minutes and add a little each week, aiming for about 30 minutes on most days. It is normal to feel a little out of breath, but you should still be able to talk.

## When to stop
Stop and rest if you feel dizzy, have chest pain, or are much more breathless than usual. Seek urgent help if chest pain or severe breathlessness does not settle with rest.
