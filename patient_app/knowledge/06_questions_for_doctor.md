title: Questions you can ask your doctor
source: Prepared from general patient-communication guidance (shared decision-making materials)

## About your result
How big is the nodule and where is it? Is it solid or ground-glass? Has it changed compared with any earlier scan? How worried should I be?

## About the next steps
When should I have my next scan? Do I need any other tests, like a PET-CT or a biopsy? What would make you change the plan?

## About my health
Does my smoking history, family history or work exposure change my risk? What can I do to keep my lungs healthy? Can you help me stop smoking?

## Practical questions
Who do I contact if I get new symptoms? How will I receive my next results?
