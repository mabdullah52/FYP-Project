title: Looking after your lungs
source: Summary of WHO information on air pollution and general lung-health advice

## Air pollution
On days with heavy air pollution or smog, limit time and exercise outdoors, keep windows closed during peak pollution hours, and consider a well-fitting mask (such as an N95) if you must be outside. Avoid burning wood, rubbish or charcoal indoors and make sure kitchens are well ventilated.

## Everyday habits
Eat plenty of fruit and vegetables, stay physically active, keep vaccinations such as influenza and pneumococcal up to date as advised by your doctor, and protect yourself from dust and fumes at work.
