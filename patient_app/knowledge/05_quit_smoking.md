title: Stopping smoking - benefits and help
source: Summary of World Health Organization (WHO) information on the health benefits of quitting tobacco

## Why quitting helps at any age
Stopping smoking is the most important thing you can do for your lungs. The benefits start quickly. Within about 20 minutes heart rate and blood pressure drop. Within 12 hours the carbon monoxide level in the blood returns to normal. Within 2 to 12 weeks circulation improves and lung function increases. Within 1 to 9 months coughing and shortness of breath decrease. After 1 year the risk of coronary heart disease is about half that of a smoker. After 10 years the risk of dying from lung cancer is about half that of a smoker.

## How to quit
Choose a quit date and tell family and friends. Remove cigarettes, lighters and ashtrays from your home and car. Avoid triggers such as tea breaks with smokers at first. Ask your doctor about nicotine replacement (patches, gum) or medicines - they roughly double the chance of success. If you slip, do not give up: most people need several tries.

## Second-hand smoke and other smoke
Avoid other people's smoke and do not allow smoking at home. Shisha (hookah) and smokeless tobacco are also harmful.
