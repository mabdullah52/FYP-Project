title: Lung cancer screening
source: Summary of the US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF, 2021) recommendation on lung cancer screening

## Who is usually advised to have yearly screening?
Adults aged 50 to 80 years who have a 20 pack-year smoking history and currently smoke or have quit within the past 15 years. A pack-year means smoking one pack (20 cigarettes) a day for one year.

## Why screening helps
Yearly low-dose CT finds lung cancer earlier, when treatment works better. The large NLST trial showed fewer deaths from lung cancer in people screened with low-dose CT.

## Downsides
Screening can find harmless spots that need extra scans, and there is a small amount of radiation. That is why it is offered to people at higher risk.
