title: When to get urgent help
source: General emergency-symptom guidance for people with lung conditions

## Seek emergency care now if you have
Coughing up blood, chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath or breathlessness at rest, fainting, blue lips, or sudden confusion.

## Contact your doctor soon if you have
A new cough lasting more than three weeks, unexplained weight loss, a fever that does not settle, a hoarse voice for more than three weeks, or tiredness that keeps getting worse.
